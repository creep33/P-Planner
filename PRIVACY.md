# P-Planner Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 14, 2026_

P-Planner does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

## What is stored

The project names and tracked times you enter are saved with the browser's `chrome.storage.sync` API.
That data stays in your browser profile. If you have turned on your browser's sync (Chrome Sync or
Brave Sync), the browser syncs it to your own devices under your browser's sync settings and privacy policy.

The developer has no server and cannot access, read or recover your data.

## What is not done

- No analytics, tracking or telemetry.
- No network requests of any kind.
- No access to your browsing history, tabs or page content.
- No remote code.

## Permissions

- `storage`: saves your projects and tracked time, and syncs them across your own devices.

## Export and import

**Export** downloads a JSON file to your computer. **Import** reads a JSON file you select. Both happen
locally. Nothing is uploaded.

## Removing your data

Delete projects from the extension, or uninstall the extension to remove its stored data.
